# RDCM Code of Conduct

RDCM welcomes questions, bug reports, reviews and contributions from people of all backgrounds and experience levels. This code applies to everyone who participates in RDCM’s GitHub spaces, including users, contributors and the maintainer.

## Community standards

- Treat people with respect, give constructive feedback and keep disagreements focused on the work.
- Harassment (including unwanted sexual attention), discrimination, personal attacks, threats and retaliation for reporting a concern are unacceptable.
- Respect others’ privacy. Share only material you have permission to share. Before posting screenshots, logs or exported connection profiles, redact credentials, usernames, hostnames, IP addresses and other private details.

## Reporting concerns

Use **Report content** on the relevant post and choose **Report to repository admins** if GitHub offers that option. If it’s unavailable or the concern involves the maintainer, [report abuse privately to GitHub Support](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

Include a short description and any relevant links in your private report.

## Enforcement

The maintainer will review reports they receive and share details only as needed to address the concern. Depending on the severity and whether the behaviour is repeated, the maintainer may issue a warning, remove content, lock a conversation or block participation in the repository.
